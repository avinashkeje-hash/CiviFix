CivicFixSewage & drainage grievance platform

Report a sewage or drainage problem. See who is fixing it, and by when.

Every complaint gets a tracking ID, a response deadline based on severity, an assigned crew and a public record of each step.

How a complaint is handled

  1. SubmittedLogged with a deadline: 6 hours for emergencies, 24 for high, 72 for normal.
  2. Under ReviewA ward officer verifies it and ranks it by severity, age and residents affected.
  3. In ProgressA crew is assigned and you are notified.
  4. ResolvedThe officer records what was done. You can rate the fix or reopen it.
